Audio Studio Tycoon is an in-depth game development tycoon simulation specifically built from the ground up for blind, visually impaired, and sighted players alike.

Features:
- 100% Accessibility: Full support for TalkBack and screen readers
- Comprehensive audio cues, speech synthesis, and tactile feedback
- Build and manage your video game development studio from 1980 through modern eras
- Research innovative gaming tech, custom engines, and platforms
- Hire staff, manage burnout, market releases, and conquer the charts
- Completely ad-free, tracker-free, and open source
